### CHEM-000448: Molecular identity of emerald-green photo-oxidation products
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Determine stoichiometry, coordination, hydration, and connectivity of the amorphous As(V)-rich products formed during illumination. Distinguish copper arsenates, mixed-cation products, and organically associated arsenate.
- **Status:** A 2025 study separated light-driven surface oxidation from humidity-driven arsenolite formation. It explicitly left the structure and stoichiometry of the predominantly amorphous As(V) products unresolved.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Product identity determines whether arsenic remains immobilized or migrates.
- **Key works:** S. Carboni Marri et al. (2025), “Discovering the dual degradation pathway of emerald green in oil paints: The effects of light and humidity,” [DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.ady1807](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.ady1807).
- **Related:** Environmental geology—arsenate release from disordered solids.
